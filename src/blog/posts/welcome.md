# Welcome

I'm Daniel Laskewitz, Principal Cloud Advocate at Microsoft. I work on the Power
Platform and Copilot Studio, and I spend a lot of the year on stage talking about
agents, MCP and low-code development.

This site is where all of that lives.

## What's here

**[Events](/events/)** is the speaking record — everywhere I've spoken since 2022,
and where I'm heading next. **[Talks](/talks/)** is the other half of the same
story: the sessions themselves, who I give them with, and which events each one
has been given at. Most of my talks aren't one-offs. They get given again, often
with a different person next to me on stage.

Writing is this part. Notes when I have something worth writing down, not on a
schedule.

## If you're here from a QR code

Then you probably want a resource page, and you don't want to read any of this.

Those pages live at `/r/<talk>` and they're built for exactly one moment: you're
in a session room that's already emptying, on conference wifi, holding your phone
up at my last slide. They're deliberately plain. Every link from the session,
plus how to reach me and whoever I presented with.

The co-speakers on those pages come from the URL, which means the QR code on my
slides never has to change when I give the same talk with someone else. That was
the whole reason I rebuilt this site.

## A feed, and nothing else

There's an [RSS feed](/feed.rss). No newsletter, no signup, no account, no popup
asking for your email address. If you want to know when I write something, your
reader will tell you.

If one of these talks fits an event you're organising, or you just want to argue
about whether MCP is worth the hype, I'm easiest to reach on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laskewitz/).
